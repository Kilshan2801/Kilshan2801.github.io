project/02 · featuredfull-stack

AgriConnect — Farmer Support & Marketplace

A web platform that brings farmers, buyers, agricultural experts and equipment providers together in one place, with an admin panel to run it. Designed as an HCI-focused project with attention to usability, feedback, error prevention and accessibility.

  • Farmers list crops, process orders, book expert consultations and rent equipment
  • Buyers browse the marketplace, search crops by text or image, and track orders
  • Experts run consultations, chat with farmers and share curated resources
  • Equipment providers manage listings and rental requests
  • Admins approve registrations, manage users, resolve conflicts and review reports

Every role gets messaging, notifications, history and settings, and users with several roles can switch between them. Logins use hashed passwords and JWT tokens.
